TrustFun

TrustFun is a financial planning and savings system designed for long-term goal tracking, budgeting, and structured capital allocation with optional Bitcoin integration.

TrustFun is a planning ledger, not an investment adviser or trading interface. Bitcoin price references are manually entered; no live market feed, custody, or order execution is claimed.
