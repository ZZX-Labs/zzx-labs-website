LightningLotto

LightningLotto is a high-frequency, sats-denominated Bitcoin lottery engine operating on fixed UTC time slots. It extends the BitLotto deterministic drawing model with rapid cadence rounds, Lightning-friendly payout logic, and reproducible fairness proofs anchored to Bitcoin block data. Designed for transparency, verifiability, and server-side orchestration, LightningLotto supports GUI, CLI, and API-driven workflows.
