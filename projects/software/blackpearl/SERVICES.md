# BlackPearl Services

BlackPearl supports a BTC-only custom-development offering for independent creators and studios.

## Custom Websites

Creator-owned domains, portfolios, blogs, galleries, publishing systems, membership interfaces, private media workflows, and custom branded sites.

## Custom Software

Desktop/server utilities, media processing, archive tools, workflow automation, creator administration, privacy utilities, and custom operational software.

## Custom Applications

Android only.

Native Android creator companions, capture tools, publishing workflows, private administration tools, and related applications.

## Commercial structure

- Bitcoin only.
- One-time scoped project fee.
- Additional continuation fees when the project grows in size, complexity, traffic, maintenance, or requested scope.
- No automatic entitlement to indefinite support.
- Final requirements and fees belong in a written scope.
- ZZX-Labs may refuse a project.

## Client-provided materials

The client supplies:

- domain;
- hosting requirements where relevant;
- media/content;
- written copy;
- fonts and licenses;
- color scheme;
- icons;
- logos;
- branding;
- rights/releases/licenses for supplied material.

Do not send seed phrases, private keys, wallet passwords, or unrelated credentials as part of a project handoff.
