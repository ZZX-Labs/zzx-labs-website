ZZX-BCS

ZZX-BCS (Billing & Compensation System) is an internal, private financial management system for ZZX-Labs used to track billing, compensation, retainers, expenses, budgets, and treasury flows across projects and personnel.

INTERNAL – Private financial system for internal operations only. The browser build is a local financial operations ledger. It uses manually entered values and does not claim live exchange rates, tax calculations, custody, payroll compliance, or bank/payment integrations.
