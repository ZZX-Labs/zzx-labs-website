FEDERATED BIODIVERSITY KNOWLEDGE NETWORK

SpeciedexNet

SpeciedexNet is a federated P2P knowledge network for distributed biodiversity research. It enables signed, versioned contributions, encrypted field communications, and conservation telemetry linked to market incentives.

The browser companion models peers, signed contributions, encrypted field messages, and Bitcoin incentive metadata. It does not open real P2P connections or execute Lightning/Bitcoin payments.
