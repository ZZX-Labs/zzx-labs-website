GKSs (Glyph Key Sprites)

GKSs are animated digital sentinels bound to Bitcoin and cryptainer keys. Each sprite represents an encrypted container’s guardian entity—generated through random dice-based entropy and procedural pixel art. Traits, rarity, and behavior correspond to wallet tiers, enabling visual cryptography and gamified key management.
