Audio Soundboard System for ZZX-Labs — a local-first pad-based audio launcher for clips, cues, samples, alerts, and voice-system outputs. Supports per-pad volume, looping, hotkeys, banks, waveform-aware media loading, recording/export workflows, and integration points for ZZX-STT, ZZX-TTS, and ZZX-VCS.

INTERNAL – Not for public distribution. Clips are selected and played locally; nothing is uploaded automatically.
