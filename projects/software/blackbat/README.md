# BlackBat

**INTERNAL – Not for public distribution.**

Bitcoin trading-algorithm research suite (BATS/ATSP) focused on long-term BTC accumulation, position sizing, drawdown limits, and multi-venue risk controls.

**Version:** 0.1.0-alpha  
**License:** MIT

The browser edition is a local research simulator with CSV market data, BATS/ATSP/DCA strategy models, portfolio drawdown stops, per-bar deployment limits, venue-allocation checks, backtesting, and research export.

No exchange credentials or live-order submission are implemented.

Deploy directly into `/projects/software/blackbat/`.

`logo.png` is intentionally omitted so existing site artwork is not overwritten.
