# BitTrader

Algorithmic Bitcoin trading research and backtesting platform.

**Version:** 0.8.0-alpha  
**License:** MIT

The browser edition supports OHLCV CSV import, SMA crossover, RSI mean-reversion and buy-and-hold simulations, fee-aware backtests, equity curves, return/drawdown/trade metrics, market-condition analysis, controlled public ticker polling, and research export.

It does not authenticate to trading accounts or place orders.

Deploy directly into `/projects/software/bittrader/`.

`logo.png` is intentionally omitted until final artwork is supplied.
