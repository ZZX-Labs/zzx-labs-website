STOA

STOA (Savings Timelock-Out Accounts) provides Bitcoin policy templates, locking scripts, and auditable dashboards for delayed spending and escrow-based time-locked savings instruments.

The static page is a noncustodial policy/script-template planner. It never asks for private keys, seed phrases, wallet passwords, RPC credentials, or macaroons and does not sign or broadcast transactions.
