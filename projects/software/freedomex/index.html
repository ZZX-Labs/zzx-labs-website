THE DISTINCTION THAT MATTERS

Coordination may centralize. Bitcoin authority does not.

01

User-controlled signer

The participant creates and protects the spending key outside FreedomEx. The application never asks for a seed phrase, private key or wallet backup.

02

CEX or DEX coordination

A CEX sequences and matches order intents under one operator. A DEX distributes signed offers through replaceable relays. Neither receives spending authority.

03

Unsigned settlement plan

Matching produces terms and a proposed PSBT or Lightning workflow—not a custodial withdrawal command and not an opaque request to sign.

04

Independent verification

Participants verify amount, fee, outputs, change and timeout conditions on independent signing devices before Bitcoin settlement can occur.

CEX

Centralized market, noncustodial settlement

The operator can run identity, policy, market data, sequencing and matching. It cannot turn an administrator login, employee phone or server credential into authority over participant Bitcoin.

COMMON DEFENSE BASELINE

What every FreedomEx port must preserve

01

Bitcoin only

BTC paired with USD or CAD for research. No token, stablecoin, wrapped asset or altcoin support.

02

No singular keyholder

No one employee, phone, administrator, workload or signer can authorize material Bitcoin movement.

03

No internal-network trust

Every human and service authenticates. An internal IP address is never proof of identity or permission.

04

Source is not a secret boundary

Repositories contain no live key material. Disclosure must not expose credentials capable of moving funds.

05

Independent output review

Signers display and verify the complete transaction, not an unexplained digest supplied by a front end.

06

Recoverable evidence

Approvals, policy decisions and release provenance survive destruction of the production environment.

NOBITEX CASE STUDY

Learn from the breach without copying the leak

The four editions use public incident analysis to model defensive boundaries. They contain no Nobitex source. Evidence, victim statements, attacker statements, inference and unknowns are kept separate in each edition’s reports.

Public evidence does not establish the proposed phone-takeover theory or a precise BTC-versus-altcoin loss split. FreedomEx still designs against device takeover because a safe exchange must tolerate it whether or not it caused this incident.

BRANDED SOURCE NETWORK

Free with the national satire intact

FreedomGewse is the Canadian branch. FreedomKobra is the United States branch. Community use preserves the complete national branding, attribution and designated territory. White-label, no-attribution and outside-territory editions require written authorization or a commercial contract.

Those field-of-use, territory and branding conditions make the suite source-available software—not OSI-approved open source.

BROWSER SECURITY BOUNDARY

FreedomEx routes research. It does not route money.
