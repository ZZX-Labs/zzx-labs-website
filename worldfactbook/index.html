One project / four views

The ZZX-WorldFactbook Family

Each layer expands the previous one. Source boundaries remain visible so a reader can always tell what is historical CIA material, what is a British supplement, what comes from global sources, and what is model-assisted synthesis.

01

Historical Core

ZZX-WorldFactbook

A provenance-separated archive of World Factbook material from 1962 through the CIA web-era, plus manually entered continuation/print editions through 2027, preserving country profiles, reference data, leaders, maps, imagery, archive editions, Fact of the Day, Image of the Day, and related public features wherever recoverable.

  • Edition-by-edition archive
  • Country and territory profiles
  • World leaders / heads of state
  • Historic Fact of the Day
  • Historic Image of the Day
  • OCR, media, citations, provenance
Open historical layer
02

British Supplement

ZZX-BritishWorldFactbook

The historical core plus distinctly British public-source intelligence and reference material: UK Government, FCDO, MoD, GCHQ, MI5, SIS/MI6, ONS, Parliament, The National Archives, UK defence history, and relevant NATO/UN material.

  • UK source overlays and comparisons
  • British geopolitical and defence context
  • British historical IC lore for daily facts
  • NATO and UN public reference material
  • Original source attribution retained
03

Global Reference Layer

ZZX-GlobalWorldFactbook

The historical and British layers expanded with public data from international institutions, national statistical offices, central banks, foreign ministries, defence publications, and source-labeled material from allied, neutral, and adversarial states.

  • UN / World Bank / IMF / NATO
  • National official-source layers
  • Historical blocs including USSR-era data
  • Cross-source conflict and revision tracking
  • Source lineage visible at every layer
04

TensorFlow Synthesis Layer

ZZX-HybridWorldFactbook

A provenance-first synthesis environment that lets a user choose time range, geography, source families, historical depth, and output style. TensorFlow models can reproduce the structure and editorial character of the archive while factual claims remain grounded in cited data.

  • User-defined source and time filters
  • Historical style model + current data retrieval
  • Generated text clearly labeled
  • Per-claim citations and model versioning
  • No silent blending of source provenance

British Variant

ZZX-BritishWorldFactbook

A British counterpart should feel genuinely British rather than being a renamed copy. The CIA archive remains intact underneath it; British material is a supplemental source layer with its own citations, dates, institutions, and historical framing.

Candidate public-source families include GOV.UK, FCDO, Ministry of Defence, GCHQ, MI5, SIS/MI6, Office for National Statistics, Parliament, The National Archives, public defence histories, NATO, and UN publications.

Global Variant

ZZX-GlobalWorldFactbook

The Global layer is a source federation, not a flattening exercise. Conflicting numbers, dates, definitions, and political descriptions should remain attributable to the institution that published them instead of being silently averaged into a single unexplained answer.

Long-run coverage can extend toward 1962 wherever archives permit, with explicit gaps rather than synthetic backfilling.

Data Architecture

        
Archive → Normalize → Cite → Publish

      

      

        The page is a static client. The crawler does the expensive archival work; GitHub Pages or nginx
        serves the normalized public output at effectively zero application-hosting cost.
      

    


    

      

        01
        Acquire
        
Historical editions, web captures, books, documents, media, and official public data.

      

      

        02
        Normalize
        
Extract text, OCR images, crop media, identify categories, preserve dates and source hashes.

      

      

        03
        Attribute
        
Keep CIA/source credit, third-party image credits, archive identifiers, filenames, and citation keys.

      

      

        04
        Shard
        
Produce bounded JSON/media contracts and MariaDB-compatible historical shards.

      

      

        05
        Publish
        
Serve a public static portal with no account requirement and no proprietary reader.
