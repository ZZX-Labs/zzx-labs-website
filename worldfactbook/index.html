ABOUT THIS PORTAL

One historical interface, sixty-four editions.

ZZX-Labs preserves the archived public-domain World Factbook corpus as structured, edition-specific data. Original source provenance remains attached to every normalized text chunk and recovered image.

OCR and image-recognition metadata improve discovery, but do not replace historical attribution. Where the source does not contain a recoverable image credit, this portal says so explicitly.
